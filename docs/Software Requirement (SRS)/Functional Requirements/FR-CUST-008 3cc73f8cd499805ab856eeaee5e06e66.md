## Overview

This requirement defines the process that allows an authenticated customer to view products they have saved to their wishlist.

The wishlist provides customers with a persistent collection of products they may want to consider purchasing later.

## Description

The system shall allow an authenticated customer to view their wishlist.

The system shall retrieve wishlist items associated with the authenticated customer's account and display the associated product information.

The system shall ensure that customers can only access their own wishlist.

## Actors

| Actor Role | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Customer** | Opens and views their saved wishlist products. |
| **System** | Authenticates the customer, retrieves the customer's wishlist, and returns the associated product information. |

## Preconditions

- The customer has an existing account.
- The customer is authenticated.
- The authentication session is valid.
- The associated customer account exists.
- The system can access the database.
- Product information required to display wishlist items is available.

## Trigger

The customer opens the **Wishlist** section of the Customer Portal.

## Main Flow

1. The customer opens the Customer Portal.
2. The customer selects **Wishlist**.
3. The system verifies that the customer has a valid authentication session.
4. The system identifies the authenticated customer.
5. The system retrieves the wishlist associated with the customer account.
6. The system retrieves the product information associated with the wishlist items.
7. The system returns the wishlist data.
8. The system displays the saved products to the customer.

## Alternative Flow

### A1 — Authentication Required

- The customer does not have a valid authentication session.
- The system rejects the wishlist request.
- The customer is redirected to the login flow.

### A2 — Empty Wishlist

- The customer does not have any saved wishlist items.
- The system returns an empty wishlist collection.
- The system displays an appropriate empty state.
- The customer can continue browsing products.

### A3 — Product No Longer Available

- A wishlist item references a product that is no longer available through the marketplace.
- The system handles the unavailable product according to the configured product availability policy.
- The remaining valid wishlist items remain accessible.

### A4 — Customer Account Not Found

- The authenticated session references a customer account that is no longer available.
- The system rejects the request.
- No wishlist data is returned.

### A5 — Wishlist Retrieval Failure

- The system cannot retrieve wishlist data from the database.
- The system returns an appropriate error.
- Wishlist contents are not presented as successfully loaded.

## Postconditions

### If Successful

- The customer's wishlist is retrieved.
- The customer can view their saved products.
- No other customer's wishlist is exposed.

### If Wishlist Is Empty

- An empty wishlist state is displayed.
- The customer can continue browsing or add products to the wishlist.

### If Failed

- No wishlist information belonging to other customers is exposed.
- No wishlist data is modified.

## Business Rules

| Rule ID | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **BR-CUST-036** | Only an authenticated customer can access their wishlist. |
| **BR-CUST-037** | A customer can only view wishlist items associated with their own account. |
| **BR-CUST-038** | Wishlist items must reference products available through the marketplace product model. |
| **BR-CUST-039** | An empty wishlist is a valid customer state. |
| **BR-CUST-040** | Wishlist access must not expose another customer's saved products. |

## Validation Rules

| Field | Validation |
| --- | --- |
| **Authentication Session** | Must be valid and associated with an existing customer. |
| **Customer Identity** | Must correspond to the authenticated customer. |
| **Wishlist Items** | Must belong to the authenticated customer's wishlist. |

## Acceptance Criteria

- An authenticated customer can open their wishlist.
- An unauthenticated customer cannot access the wishlist.
- The system displays products saved by the authenticated customer.
- A customer cannot view another customer's wishlist.
- An empty wishlist displays an appropriate empty state.
- Wishlist retrieval does not modify wishlist data.
- Unavailable products are handled according to the configured product availability policy.
