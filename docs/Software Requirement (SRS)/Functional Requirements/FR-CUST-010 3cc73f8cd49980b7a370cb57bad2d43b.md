## Overview

This requirement defines the process that allows an authenticated customer to remove a previously saved product from their wishlist.

Removing a product shall update the customer's wishlist without affecting the product itself or the wishlist of another customer.

## Description

The system shall allow an authenticated customer to remove a product from their wishlist.

The system shall verify that the wishlist item belongs to the authenticated customer before removing it.

The system shall not modify the product record when a wishlist item is removed.

## Actors

| Actor Role | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Customer** | Selects a saved product and removes it from their wishlist. |
| **System** | Authenticates the customer, verifies wishlist ownership, removes the wishlist item, and returns the result. |

## Preconditions

- The customer has an existing account.
- The customer is authenticated.
- The authentication session is valid.
- The associated customer account exists.
- The selected product exists or the corresponding wishlist item exists.
- The system can access the database.

## Trigger

The customer selects **Remove from Wishlist** for a saved product.

## Main Flow

1. The customer opens the **Wishlist**.
2. The system displays the customer's saved wishlist products.
3. The customer selects a product to remove.
4. The customer selects **Remove from Wishlist**.
5. The system verifies the customer's authentication session.
6. The system identifies the selected wishlist item.
7. The system verifies that the wishlist item belongs to the authenticated customer.
8. The system removes the wishlist item.
9. The system persists the change.
10. The system returns a successful response.
11. The product is removed from the customer's wishlist display.

## Alternative Flow

### A1 — Authentication Required

- The customer does not have a valid authentication session.
- The system rejects the request.
- The wishlist is not modified.

### A2 — Wishlist Item Not Found

- The selected wishlist item does not exist.
- The system rejects or safely handles the request according to the configured behavior.
- No unrelated wishlist item is modified.

### A3 — Wishlist Item Does Not Belong to Customer

- The selected wishlist item belongs to another customer.
- The system rejects the request.
- No wishlist data belonging to another customer is exposed or modified.

### A4 — Wishlist Removal Failure

- The system cannot persist the removal.
- The system returns an appropriate error response.
- The wishlist item remains unchanged.

## Postconditions

### If Successful

- The selected product is no longer present in the customer's wishlist.
- Other wishlist items remain unchanged.
- The product itself is not modified.

### If Failed

- The selected wishlist item remains unchanged.
- No unrelated wishlist data is modified.

## Business Rules

| Rule ID | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **BR-CUST-047** | Only an authenticated customer can remove products from their wishlist. |
| **BR-CUST-048** | A customer can only remove wishlist items belonging to their own wishlist. |
| **BR-CUST-049** | Removing a wishlist item must not modify the associated product record. |
| **BR-CUST-050** | Removing one wishlist item must not affect unrelated wishlist items. |
| **BR-CUST-051** | A wishlist item that does not belong to the authenticated customer must not be modified. |

## Validation Rules

| Field | Validation |
| --- | --- |
| **Authentication Session** | Must be valid and associated with an existing customer. |
| **Wishlist Item** | Must reference an existing wishlist item. |
| **Wishlist Ownership** | Must correspond to the authenticated customer. |

## Acceptance Criteria

- An authenticated customer can remove a saved product from their wishlist.
- An unauthenticated customer cannot remove a wishlist item.
- A customer can only remove items from their own wishlist.
- Removing a wishlist item does not modify the underlying product.
- Other wishlist items remain unchanged.
- A successful removal is reflected in the customer's Wishlist.
- An invalid or unauthorized removal does not modify another customer's wishlist.
